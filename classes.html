Levels 1–3

Aerial Silks

Two lengths of fabric, and a slow build toward flight. Every level keeps shoulder health and core control front and center.

Level 1Hand, shoulder and core strength. The foundation for effortless inversions and endurance.
Level 2Flexibility and joint control, with an introduction to spinning and drops.
Level 3Balance and side rotations as drops and dives get more complex.
All Levels

Two more ways to build strength off the ground.

Lyra & Trapeze

Basic skills that build strength, flexibility and grace on the hoop and bar. Spinning, body lifting, climbing, inversions, rolling and turning, with correct form and safety always first.

Ankle and knee protection available in studio.

Handbalancing

Proper technique for wrist and spine health while building the strength for inversions, handstands and cane work. The quietest apparatus in the building, and one of the hardest.

Aerial Yoga

Acrobatic elements meet alignment-focused yoga, using a fabric hammock for deeper stretches and inversions without strain on the joints. No prior experience required, when it's running.
