Email Kids' Program
Ages 6–12

Built for kids, not shrunk down from adults.

Our kids' classes teach real aerial skills on silks, sling and lyra, but the real work happens underneath: creativity, body awareness and the kind of confidence that comes from doing something a little bit scary and sticking the landing.

What To Expect

The details that make parents comfortable.

Small & Focused

Max 6 kids per class, so every child gets real time on the apparatus, not just a turn in line.

Safety First

High-density gymnastic mats under every apparatus, every class, no exceptions.

One Hour, Weekly

Semester-based classes build skills gradually across the year, all year round.

What To Wear

Comfortable clothing that covers knees and shoulders. No zippers or buttons. Yoga pants and a t-shirt are perfect.

Enrollment

A semester-long adventure.

Kids' classes run in four semesters a year, winter, spring, summer and fall, with sign-ups opening about a month before each one starts. Enrollment is $75 a month with automatic renewal, so your spot carries through the whole semester. Summer sessions sometimes have more openings for kids joining mid-year.
