How We Teach

Three things we care about more than tricks.

01

Safety

We build a slow, sustainable foundation before anything flashy. Conditioning and technique come first, because that's what makes the fun part safe.

02

Smiles

Circus arts are for your body, your mind and your heart. Class stays constructive, non-competitive and genuinely friendly, a real break from the outside world.

03

Skills

You'll move through clear, linear steps, building strength, flexibility, balance and endurance before we ever chase the aesthetic stuff.

What We Teach

Four ways to fly, plus a fifth for the kids.

Levels 1–3

Aerial Silks

Climb, wrap and drop through fabric. Build the strength and form for effortless inversions, then chase spins and dives as you advance.

 All Levels

Lyra & Trapeze

Spinning, climbing, inversions and turns on the hoop and bar. Correct form and safety first, grace and style close behind.

 All Levels

Handbalancing

Wrist and spine health, canes, and the strength to hold a handstand like you mean it.

 Ages 6–12

The Studio

North Carolina's first circus arts studio, still full of surprises.

Founded in 2009, Aerial Space has spent over fifteen years building a home for anyone curious enough to try flying. Our instructors come from dance, gymnastics, theater and everywhere in between, brought together by the same childlike joy of hanging upside down.

Meet the Instructors

Fifty-five and just getting started.

Ageless Aerials is built for adventurous students 55 and up: gentle progressions, real strength work, and an instructor who checks in with you at the start of every class. No aerial background required, just curiosity.
