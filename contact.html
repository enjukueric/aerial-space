Getting Here

Easy to find, even on the first try.

From Patton Avenue, turn onto New Leicester Highway and get into the far left lane. Go about 800 feet, then turn left onto Mooseheart Lane. Aerial Space is the first big building on the left, you can't miss the silks hanging in the windows.
