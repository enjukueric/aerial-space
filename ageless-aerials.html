How It Works

Aerial arts, adapted to you.

Every Ageless Aerials class starts the same way: a quick, genuine check-in about how your body and mind are doing that day. From there, your instructor adapts the curriculum to you, building hand strength and coordination gradually, the same way a good yoga teacher would work around a bad knee or a stiff shoulder.

No aerial background required. No particular fitness level required either. Just a willingness to try something you probably didn't expect to be doing at this age.

What Makes It Different

Patient, adapted, and genuinely social.

Gentle Progressions

Skills build slowly and safely, with real strength and balance work underneath every class.

Individual Check-Ins

Your instructor asks how you're doing, physically and mentally, before every single class.

All Abilities Welcome

Curriculum adapts around health restrictions the same way a thoughtful yoga class would.
